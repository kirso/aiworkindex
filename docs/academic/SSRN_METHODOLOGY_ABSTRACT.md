# SSRN Methodology Abstract Draft

## Title

AI Work Index: A Reproducible Structural Exposure Index for AI Pressure in Singapore Occupations

## Abstract

This paper describes the AI Work Index, a deterministic occupational index of structural AI
displacement pressure for Singapore. The index combines four complementary exposure sources with
human bottleneck measures, task-concentration adjustment, local demand-resilience signals, and
published labour-market context. It is designed as an exposure and pressure measure, not a forecast
of job loss.

The methodology separates technical exposure from realised labour-market outcomes. It reports
uncertainty ranges, crosswalk quality, validation limits, and known labour-economics caveats,
including hiring-margin effects, wage and role-redesign channels, Singapore's foreign-workforce
buffer, and the distinction between structural transition adjacency and retraining efficacy.

The release includes convergent checks against U.S. BLS 2024-2034 projected employment growth,
Singapore IMF exposure/complementarity estimates, cluster-level labour-monitor diagnostics, and
sensitivity analysis over the hand-set constants. Under joint perturbation of ranking constants,
rankings remain highly stable while absolute band labels are more level-sensitive, especially to
demand-axis assumptions.

All score computations are reproducible from public inputs through an open-source pipeline. Public
artifacts include occupation-level JSON/CSV exports, release manifests with checksums, validation
artifacts, source-map documentation, and versioned methodology pages.
